# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are **non-technical professionals** — doctors, business owners, daily-wage workers, and similar — not developers or power users. They need to capture, organize, and recall their knowledge, automate routine work, and get small tools built for them, without writing code or configuring integrations. Low digital-literacy barrier is a hard requirement, not a nicety.

## Product Purpose

twodb is a **second brain**: one product that combines

- notes and docs (closest reference: Notion),
- linked knowledge (Obsidian-like),
- workflow automation (n8n-like),
- an intelligent AI/LLM layer with a chat system,
- an AI app builder (opencode-like) that creates small apps for the user,

and more, unified in a single place. Success means a non-technical user can dump their thinking into twodb, find it again effortlessly, and have the product act on it — reminding, automating, answering, and building — without ever touching a developer tool.

## Positioning

Neighboring tools each serve one slice and assume technical comfort: Notion (docs, no real automation or AI agency), Obsidian (linked notes, plugin-technical), n8n (automation, node-graph-technical), opencode (app building, developer-only). twodb's claim none of them can truthfully copy: **the full second-brain stack — capture, connect, automate, chat, and build — designed for people who are not technical at all.**

## Operating Context

Web app used in daily knowledge-work and business situations: quick capture during a busy day, recalling information on demand, chatting with the AI about one's own data, setting up automations in plain language, and getting small purpose-built apps generated. Monorepo: `apps/web` (main app), `apps/api` (Fastify backend), `apps/ui-library` (component showcase), `packages/ui` (shared components).

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed intended capabilities: notes/docs, linked knowledge, automation, AI chat, AI app builder; scope deliberately broader ("much more") and still open. Constraints:

- Must be approachable to non-technical users — plain language over jargon everywhere.
- No existing brand assets, legal commitments, or locked decisions; "everything is open."

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **twodb**.
- Binding visual constraint (user-volunteered): calm UI, slightly condensed density with good negative space, **no heavy glow or heavy shadows**. Closest UI reference: Notion.

## Evidence on Hand

None. No content, testimonials, customers, benchmarks, or imagery exist yet — future work must not fabricate any.

## Product Principles

1. **Non-technical first.** If a doctor or shop owner can't do it without help, the feature isn't done.
2. **One brain, many skills.** Notes, automation, chat, and apps share the same data and context; nothing lives in a silo.
3. **Calm capability.** Power is revealed progressively; default surfaces stay simple and quiet.
4. **Open by default.** No premature commitments — technical or brand — until real usage demands them.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Audience spans wide ranges of age, language, and digital literacy: plain language, generous legibility, and forgiving interactions are product requirements.
